# salesprediction
This project involves building a simple yet effective regression model to predict product sales based on advertising expenditure across three media channels: TV, Radio, and Newspaper.
Key Highlights:
Dataset: A marketing dataset containing historical data on advertising budgets and corresponding sales.
Features: TV, Radio, and Newspaper ad spending.
Target Variable: Product sales.
Model Used: LinearRegression from scikit-learn.
Data Splitting: Used an 80-20 train-test split to validate performance.
Model Evaluation:
R² Score to measure variance explained.
Mean Squared Error (MSE) to assess prediction accuracy.
New Prediction: Allows for predicting future sales by inputting new ad budgets.
Sales prediction means predicting how much of a product people will buy based on factors
such as the amount you spend to advertise your product, the segment of people you
advertise for, or the platform you are advertising on about your product.
